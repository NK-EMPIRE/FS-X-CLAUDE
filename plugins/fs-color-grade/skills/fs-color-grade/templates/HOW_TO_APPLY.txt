FIRSTSELFIE - MOODY TEAL GRADE  (one-click LUT)
================================================

FILES
  FirstSelfie_MoodyTeal_FINAL.cube   <- the complete grade in one file (65-point 3D LUT, Rec.709)
  FESTIVAL INTRO_GRADED_4K.mp4       <- graded reference video (4K, 25 fps, ~53 Mbps)
  BEFORE_AFTER_*.jpg                 <- before / after comparison
  GRADED_STILL_4K_*.png              <- exact graded 4K frames to match against

The .cube already contains EVERYTHING: exposure, contrast, white balance,
skin-tone correction and the teal look. Do not add anything else on top.

PREMIERE PRO (one click)
  1. Put the LUT on the footage clips (or on an adjustment layer above the
     footage, BELOW any titles, graphics and logo animations).
  2. Effects > Lumetri Color > Basic Correction > Input LUT > Browse...
     -> choose FirstSelfie_MoodyTeal_FINAL.cube
  3. Leave every other Lumetri slider at its default. Done.
  (The Creative > Look > Browse slot also works, with Intensity at 100.)

  To reuse it in any project with one click: after step 2, right-click
  "Lumetri Color" in Effect Controls > Save Preset... > name it
  "FS Moody Teal". It then appears under Effects > Presets.

DAVINCI RESOLVE
  Copy the .cube to Project Settings > Color Management > Open LUT Folder,
  press Update Lists, then right-click a node > LUT > FirstSelfie_MoodyTeal_FINAL.

AFTER EFFECTS
  Effect > Utility > Apply Color LUT > choose the .cube.

IMPORTANT
  - The LUT is built for the same camera footage as this rough cut
    (standard Rec.709, not LOG). Same camera, same lighting = same result.
  - Apply it only once. If the final edit already has a grade or a LUT on
    those clips, switch that off first.
  - Do not put it on graphics, text or the logo animation; only on the
    camera footage.
  - Check against GRADED_STILL_4K_*.png: the face, teal background and
    red FirstSelfie logo should match.
