# Lessons (Dhanteras Coin sound, v6 → v9)

- **"No BGM" complaints** usually mean the music is mixed too low or ducked too hard. Music at 0 dB, max -3 dB duck, LRA 2-5 LU.
- **The wrong genre for the reference:** an orchestral trailer track is not Think School. Measure the reference first (tempo, pulse, LRA, genre words) and match those.
- **Library loops fight the edit:** compose to the picture, or at least cut the track so kicks land on the 3-4 biggest cuts (search the real beat near the cut point; the tempo factor should be within ±1.5 %).
- **An orchestral bass drum alone gives no pulse** (earshot clarity 0.07). Add a pitched trailer-kick body, a pumping 8th sub and off-beat clicks: clarity 0.19, and a "heavy bass, aggressive" read.
- **A soft blast attack:** add a sharp transient (a real explosion crack) on the frame, cut the riser 80-200 ms early, and dip music/SFX/ambience before the hit.
- **The coin flip in space** used a tabletop recording. Use a zero-g Doppler spin (lp 2.5 k) plus a metal singing bowl in the score's key plus a felt sub; no landing click.
- **Openverse** API can 504 at its origin; it's not your network. Freesound needs a key. Epidemic previews (lqmp3Url) are free for auditioning.
- **earshot install:** `--index-url` (PyTorch-only) breaks build deps; use `--extra-index-url`.
- **The VSCO2 partial clone** died with "invalid index-pack": download the files from raw.githubusercontent in parallel instead.
