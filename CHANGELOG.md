# Changelog

## 2026-10-27
- **vfx-intro 1.0.0**: first release, built from the shipped *Dhanteras Coin* intro. It includes:
  - reverse-engineering analyser and idea-to-shots method;
  - Blender `vfx_lib` (relief metal, arcs, cracks, fracture, centre blast, debris, sparks, Mantaflow fire, tracking cameras, resumable renders) and a tested 10 s scene template;
  - config-driven After Effects build, relink/package script;
  - contact sheets, title PNGs, safe render launchers, 1080p/4K encodes;
  - style presets, lessons learned;
  - `/vfx-intro` and `/vfx-setup` commands.
